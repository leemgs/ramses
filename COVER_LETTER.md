# Cover letter — resubmission following TII-26-5047

Dear Editor,

Please consider our new submission, revised in response to the decision on manuscript **TII-26-5047**, “Hierarchical Memory Orchestration with RAMSES for Robust Industrial AI Inference Systems.” We fundamentally reconstructed the model and narrowed the claims. The revised model separates demand from transfer volume, represents asynchronous overlap, and removes circular thresholds and unsupported theorems. We removed all deterministic-latency, WCET, control-stability, TSN, and IEC 61508/SIL claims. We now identify the 72-hour workload as a statistically calibrated synthetic trace and scope the contribution to laboratory, single-node, best-effort inference.

We also specify the online controller’s estimator, state actions, hysteresis, parameters, objective, and complexity, and include the anonymized trace-generation artifact during review. The point-by-point response accompanying the submission identifies both completed revisions and the additional empirical materials added in the final submission package.

Sincerely,
The Authors
